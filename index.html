What matters for paediatric pneumonia detection with small CNNs?Oct 2026
MD6329 final project · PyTorch
  • Controlled study on 5,856 paediatric chest X-rays (PneumoniaMNIST): logistic regression, MLP and a plain / BatchNorm / residual CNN family at 28, 64 and 128 px, three seeds each (66 training runs).
  • Best model: test AUROC 0.989; 64 px matched 128 px at about one sixth of the compute.
  • Showed that the decision threshold, not the ranking, is the fragile part: under simulated acquisition shift, AUROC stayed at 0.98 while specificity at the deployed threshold collapsed. Grad-CAM evidence moved into the lung fields only at higher resolution.
